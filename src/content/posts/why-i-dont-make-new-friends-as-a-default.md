It’s all about goals. I’m generally goal oriented, and very focused on achieving them. Which means, I don’t create new friends or commitments that don’t serve my goals:

1. building a 7 figure SGD tuition business
2. running a 90 mins half marathon

![Abstract illustration of one person pursuing a goal apart from a group](/why-i-dont-make-new-friends.webp)

If you disagree with this, lemme say this:

> “You live your life in a way that I would not prefer” - Alex Hormozi

Which means, I live my life in a way that you would not prefer.

I do socialise by going to run clubs (which serve goal #2) and reach out to entrepreneurs to learn and seek advice (which serve goal #1). And I do catch up with friends or ex-colleagues (but not often, maybe once every 3 months or so)

This is a season of no.

Maybe in the future, I might change my mind and make new friends outside these 2 goals.

> “People… assume that if you change your mind, what you did before was wrong. But it wasn’t wrong. It was right for that time.” - Alex Hormozi
