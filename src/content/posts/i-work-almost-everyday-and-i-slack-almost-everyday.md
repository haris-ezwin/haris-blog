On average, I work 5h a day. Some days I work 3h. Some days I work 8h. The best part, I’m at peace. I used to be stressed on not working at least 8h a day. Now, it’s more like, I need to finish what I need to finish.

![Painting of an Asian businessman working peacefully on the clouds](/i-work-almost-everyday-and-i-slack-almost-everyday.webp)

Does that mean my progress is slower vs if I work 8h everyday? Of course. Life is full of phases. There are times where I will be working a lot. There are times that I won’t. And right now, its the phases of doing, but also a chill mindset.

Working a lot of hours doesn’t guarantee I would be successful. Working on the right things that move towards my goals does. So what are the right things or actions I’m working on?

For any action, it requires a goal, and right now its:

**100% of students who joined EzWin will improve by 1 grade a least.**

So the “right thing” that I’m working on:

1. A system that recognise the weaknesses of the students and create a personalised study plan
2. Create content that any student understand, and eventually, improve

Anything outside of these are a distraction.

“If you’re working all the time, and you’re not making progress, you’re doing the wrong stuff.” - Alex Hormozi
