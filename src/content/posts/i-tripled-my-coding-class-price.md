*these numbers are not exact, it’s just from memory*

In 1 day, around 20 students signed up for my intro to coding classes. In the first batch, we had 60 students.

Amazing right?

Nope.

It wasn’t making a lot of profits. Lemme prove to you:

**Revenue**<br>
→ 60 students × $180<br>
= **$10,800**

Dinner cost per lesson per student → $10

Number of lessons per student → 6

Dinner cost per student → $60

**Total dinner cost**<br>
→ 60 students × $60<br>
= **$3,600**

**Profit**<br>
→ Revenue − Total dinner cost<br>
= $10,800 − $3,600<br>
= **$7,200**

Second batch, we 3x-ed the price from $180 to $600.

Instead of 60 students, we had around 15 students.

Sounds worse right? But look at the numbers.

**Revenue**<br>
→ 15 students × $600<br>
= **$9,000**

**Total dinner cost**<br>
→ 15 students × $60<br>
= **$900**

**Profit**<br>
→ Revenue − Total dinner cost<br>
= $9,000 − $900<br>
= **$8,100**

Revenue is around the same.

**Cost: before → after**<br>
$3,600 **(−75%)** → $900

**Profits: before → after**<br>
$7,200 **(+12.5%)** → $8,100

I literally taught 75% fewer students and made more money.

This lesson has made me open my eyes to just charging more.

Make more profits + less customers = less stress and happier life.
