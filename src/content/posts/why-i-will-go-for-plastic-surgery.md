The most underrated hack for life, to me is beauty.

![Abstract painting about appearance and plastic surgery](/why-i-will-go-for-plastic-surgery.webp)

Which one will get treated better: an ugly face or a handsome face? Handsome.

Which one will have a high chance getting a partner: an ugly face or a handsome face? Handsome.

What is the disadvantage of being good looking? I genuinely don’t think there is.

What is the disadvantages of being not good looking? People will treat you not as well as you want.

Good looking face + chubby body = still attractive

Not good looking face + chubby body = not attractive

Good looking face + ripped body = very attractive

Not good looking face + ripped body = kinda attractive

Good looking face + good fashion = very attractive

Not good looking face + good fashion = okay attractive

80% of looks is your face. therefore, just optimising that will help improve a lot

If I pay $100,000 so that I can attract better partners and be treated better, for a lifetime, 100% worth.
